# JCLLLabs — Web Design System

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Purpose:** Design system based on the existing KT Soul Stylist website, modernized while preserving the luxury, minimalist, and personal styling brand identity.

---

# Brand Personality

- Elegant
- Modern
- Minimalist
- Sophisticated
- Personal
- Empowering
- Editorial
- Luxury

---

# Design Principles

1. Less is More
2. Let Photography Lead
3. High Readability
4. Plenty of White Space
5. Consistent Visual Hierarchy
6. Soft Luxury over Bold Luxury

| Name | HEX | Usage |
|------|------|------|
| Charcoal | `#2E2E2E` | Headlines, buttons, icons |
| Warm White | `#F8F6F3` | Primary background |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, sections |
